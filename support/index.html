Need help or have feedback? Email stockspeak.support@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Your memorized cards, quiz badges, and lesson position are stored locally. Deleting the app erases them.

I got a new phone. How do I get StockSpeak back?

Download it again from the App Store using the same Apple ID. You won’t be charged again. Progress does not transfer between devices.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

Audio isn’t playing.

Check that your iPhone isn’t in silent mode and the volume is up, then try closing and reopening the app.

Is this financial advice?

No. StockSpeak is an educational app that explains financial and market concepts. Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy or sell any investment.
